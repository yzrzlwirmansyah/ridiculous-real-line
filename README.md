A one-sentence pitch for a plant-spa subscription: mail your houseplants away for a week of misting, pruning and pep talks, so travelling plant parents come home to thriving leaves instead of a funeral. Ridiculous at first glance — a spa holiday for a fern — and real by the second, because people who travel already pay to keep their plants alive.

```sh
node main.ts
```
